Congress Tracker — UI directions

Three divergent takes on the same two jobs: (1) know what Congress just voted on and (2) understand how it affects real people — with built-in ways to share a vote and contact your representative. Open each on your phone and on desktop, toggle light/dark, and tell me which pieces to combine. These are throwaway HTML, not the real app.

Direction A

The Briefing

Calm, editorial, single-column morning read. Leads with a one-line "yesterday in Congress" digest, then trustworthy plain-English cards. Minimal chrome. Optimized for the daily mobile check-in.

Open Direction B

Signal Feed

Scan-first, app-like feed with sticky filter pills (House / Senate / Close votes / Topics). Compact cards expand on tap for detail + actions. "Close votes" are first-class and shareable.

Open Direction C

Impact-First

Human-centered. Each item is framed as "what changes for you," tagged by who it touches (Veterans, Renters, Students…). Action-forward: Share + Tell-your-rep are primary.

Open

What to evaluate

  • Job 1 — what passed: can you scan yesterday's results in ~10 seconds on mobile?
  • Job 2 — who it affects: does the "impact" framing feel clear and trustworthy, not preachy?
  • Close votes: are narrowly-passed votes easy to spot and share?
  • Action: are Share and Contact-your-rep obvious without cluttering the read?
  • Elections: where should "upcoming elections you can vote in" live — feed, footer, or its own tab?
